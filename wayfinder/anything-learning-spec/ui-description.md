# How the app should look — a UI description

*Derived only from the locked decisions in the tickets. Where a decision names what the learner sees, it is stated as locked. Where the decisions do not reach — colours, type, exact control shapes — it is marked **OPEN** and is not a decision. If anything here disagrees with a ticket, the ticket wins.*

## 0. Ground rules that touch every screen

- It is a browser tab on the learner's own machine, served by a local process on loopback. The page never holds the key, never constructs a prompt, never makes a provider call. ([How the learner opens the app](tickets/how-you-open-it.md))
- No scores anywhere. No percentage, no grade, no level bar, no streak. ([Reading progress back](tickets/progress-view.md))
- No nagging: no streak, no guilt, no withheld cards. "Silence belongs to the learner. This is a flashcard, not a proctor." ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md))
- No comparison to other people: no percentile, no average learner, no leaderboard, no rank. ([Reading progress back](tickets/progress-view.md))
- The outcome vocabulary is fixed: **Again / Got it / Nearly / No**, plus **not judged** as its own state. Words, never numbers. ([Evidence model](tickets/evidence-model.md), [The generation gate](tickets/generation-gate.md))
- Failures are reported in plain words, never retried silently forever, and never block work in progress. A missing key is never a startup blocker. ([Privacy boundary](tickets/privacy-boundary.md))
- The app is fully usable with no provider at all. No dark patterns around setup: with no key, studying, hand-written cards, and imports all work. ([Privacy boundary](tickets/privacy-boundary.md))
- No account, no login screen, no sharing UI, no mobile layout. Out of scope by decision. ([map](map.md))

## 1. The plan, before any card

The first thing a session shows. ([One session, end to end](tickets/session-loop.md))

- The plan and the reason, in plain words. The decision's own example of the shape: "twenty cards, sixteen from past tense because you have been getting them wrong, four from vocabulary because you are strong there and they check you are still strong".
- It shows cards, not just counts - named cards/groups, each with its reason. The app stores no reason; only shows it.
- The count is an offer, not a rule: "There is no fixed session length. The app offers a number - twenty cards as a default offer - as an offer and not a rule. The learner may stop at any point."
- Reasons may be ugly, including "no idea, these came first". That is accepted, not smoothed over.
- A way to begin. **OPEN:** the exact control.
- Nothing evaluative on this screen: no score, no timer, no streak.
- If the learner has a tutor session, this is also where the standing banner is approved — see §6. **OPEN:** whether the banner sits on the plan screen or immediately before the first card; the decision says "before the session begins".

## 2. The study screen

One card at a time. ([Evidence model](tickets/evidence-model.md), [What goes on a card](tickets/card-shape.md))

### 2.1 The card itself

- Two sides: a prompt side and an answer side. Each side is an ordered list of parts; text is the common part; any part may be an attachment (audio, image, anything a pack defines). The screen lays parts out in order and does not know what any part means.
- Tags, summary, and the origin note ride along as plain text. Origin is a note only: "written by the learner, generated by the provider, or imported".
- The full correct line is always available on a generated card: the complete thing the card was made from is present, so a wrong fact is "wrong in a way the learner can see rather than in a way they must trust". ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md), decision 13)

### 2.2 Answering happens where the question is asked

Four surfaces, all hosted by the card, all decided by the deck, card by card — never a global setting: ([How a card is answered](tickets/answering-surface.md))

- **Dotted regions**: dotted editable spaces inside a running sentence, typed in place. The dash means "edit here". Several blanks may sit on one card. **OPEN:** how many evidence rows a several-blank card writes (logged as an addendum to the evidence ticket).
- **Picks**: a fixed set, shown as a small set of choices.
- **One typed answer**: a single free-text field.
- **Hear-then-type**: audio part first, then typing.
- **OPEN:** the exact look of each surface (chip shape, blank width, audio control) — the decision fixes behavior, not styling.

### 2.3 The answering rule

"The card shows the question. The learner answers before seeing the answer… The answer is always shown." ([Evidence model](tickets/evidence-model.md))

- Answer is hidden until the learner is done; then the answer side always appears.
- Time is recorded locally and sent later, in its own batch, after the learner has answered — "never part of the live reply". So: no visible timer, no clock pressure, nothing measuring hesitation in the moment.
- Stopping is always possible; it is not an ending and produces nothing evaluative.

### 2.4 The outcome

- One of the four outcomes is recorded, always shown in the same words:

| Word | Meaning (decision text) |
|---|---|
| **Again** | wrong, but familiar. A lapse in something already known. |
| **Got it** | correct. |
| **Nearly** | substantially correct, a word or detail slipped. |
| **No** | wrong, and it was not previously known. A gap. |

- When the learner is the judge, these four words are the choices. When a judge decided, the outcome is shown with who decided it, honestly: local and free, the model, or the learner. "Nothing breaks when no judge is available. The answer still shows, the learner states the outcome." ([Evidence model](tickets/evidence-model.md))
- With judging off, the result is **not judged** — shown as its own state, never as wrong. ([The generation gate](tickets/generation-gate.md), still standing: "Judging stays configurable, per card and per pack.")

### 2.5 Hints

- A hint — offered by the tutor or asked for by the learner — caps the card at **Nearly**. The cap is shown as a fact of the record, not as a punishment. "A requested hint is capped exactly like an offered one." ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md))
- Asking is never rationed and never hidden to save money.
- A hint may be written or spoken; the pack declares which. The words always exist as text — "the voice merely reads them" — so a spoken hint is never only sound.

### 2.6 The tutor during a card

- The tutor may speak before, during, or after a card — a hint, a correction, a line of encouragement — on its own initiative. "The learner's response is the only brake." ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md))
- It talks in texts: every exchange is a message out and a message back. No open line, no waiting cursor, no "the tutor is watching".
- It cannot nag; with no provider it goes silent rather than vanishing — "silence is not a bug; it is the absence of a provider." No error banner for that.
- **OPEN:** where tutor text sits on screen (beside the card, above it, a strip).

### 2.7 Showing working

- Cards that ask for the method carry a second editable region beside the answer place: the learner shows their working. ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md), decision 17)
- After judging, the learner's route is shown back **beside** the correct one — "so they can see where it went wrong." Not folded into a score, not discarded before it is read.
- Then it is discarded: "displayed during the answer and then discarded - never stored."
- **OPEN:** the exact layout of two workings side by side.

### 2.8 Wrong cards return

- A card answered wrongly returns at the end of the same session, once. The returning cards are the same cards, plainly marked as returning. ([One session, end to end](tickets/session-loop.md))
- **OPEN:** the exact wording of "why is this back".

## 3. The ending

- "Stopping is not an ending. No summary screen, no report, no sign-off, nothing evaluative." ([One session, end to end](tickets/session-loop.md))
- When the plan runs out, the same rule holds: nothing to sign off. **OPEN:** the exact last state of a finished session, bounded by this rule.

## 4. The progress screen

The screen answers one question: "what can I do now that I could not do before." ([Reading progress back](tickets/progress-view.md))

- Named things, cards not metrics — cards "the learner used to answer wrongly that they now answer correctly, and cards that used to take them a long time that now take little".
- Each named thing carries a link to the raw evidence beneath it. Conclusions open down to evidence; evidence can be corrected with a new row; conclusions are never directly editable.
- Weakness appears as work to do: "past tense is next", never "you are bad at past tense".
- Strengths are shown, because the algorithm leans on them to keep weakness moving.
- Familiarity never appears as progress: a card answered correctly on first exposure can never show as an achievement.
- Never on this screen: scores, percentages, grades, streaks, percentiles, comparisons, leaderboards.

## 5. Packs and imports in the interface

- The library is a folder: "there is no server and nothing to browse." So no storefront, no browsing UI. ([A pack's shape](tickets/pack-shape.md))
- A broken pack is named with "plainly what is wrong with it", refused before acting, and the app continues with everything else. Not a modal wall, not a crash. ([A pack's shape](tickets/pack-shape.md))
- Import is all-or-nothing: "A malformed card rejects the whole batch with an error naming what is wrong and where." Imports arrive in chunks, not one giant paste. ([What goes on a card](tickets/card-shape.md))
- An out-of-place card "arrives flagged for the learner to accept or reject - never blocked." If no checker is available, the batch lands unflagged. **OPEN:** where the flag lives and how accept/reject looks.
- Import-without-A, I, is an open ticket: JSON file or paste, what the origin note says, checker in warning mode — all undecided.

## 6. Privacy moments in the interface

- Every outbound prompt: the learner "sees the exact prompt before it is sent, with a copy action beside the send", and the app keeps a local log of what was sent and when. ([Privacy boundary](tickets/privacy-boundary.md))
- A prompt contains only app-chosen categories — weak topics, level, learning style — and never learner-written text.
- A pack asking for the learner's own text: per-item, visible, learner selects the item and confirms that one item. "Install-time blanket consent is forbidden."
- Tutor session: one standing banner before the session shows the full shape of every prompt the tutor will send — standing request, weak topics, level, learning style, card text already decided to be shown, and the shape of the tutor's instructions — approved once. "The banner is per session and does not persist; each new session asks again."
- The key is never on this page. The page may show that a key is set or not, in plain words. ([How the learner opens the app](tickets/how-you-open-it.md))

## 7. Generated cards in the interface

- No review screen, no accept button: generated decks are "machine-validated and accepted" — well-formed output, blanks in sane positions — "and then the cards are cards." ([The A, I, tutor](tickets/ai-tutor-session.md), decision 12, recorded in the gate ticket)
- The origin note names the generation request, "so a generated card is never mistaken for a pack's own material."
- Editing makes a brand new card with zero history. **OPEN:** where editing lives.
- The three prompt slots stand as the shape of generation; the tutor's prompt is a fourth app-owned shape, recorded as owed.

## 8. Quiet receipts (settings)

- A quiet line names which algorithm ran; failures are counted; "Not an error, not a warning - just the name." ([One session, end to end](tickets/session-loop.md))
- Fallback is silent in the moment — "the card is better than the message" — and visible only here on reflection.
- Provider state in plain words: ready, not set up, or a plain explanation of failure. A failed provider never blocks work in progress.

## 9. What must never appear on any screen

Each line is a locked decision, not taste:

- No score, percentage, grade, level bar, or streak.
- No summary screen, report, or sign-off at the end of anything.
- No nag, no guilt, no streak, no withheld cards as leverage.
- No percentile, no average learner, no leaderboard, no rank.
- No review screen or accept step for generated cards.
- No key, no prompt construction, no provider call in the page.
- No learner-written text in a prompt without the learner choosing that one item.
- No blocker when a provider fails, a key is missing, a pack is broken, or a card is out of place.
- No visible timer or clock pressure.

## 10. Open — named so nobody fills it silently

From the map's "Not yet specified" and ticket addenda, everything that touches the UI:

- Deck lifecycle: create, rename, merge, delete.
- Import-without-A, I, flow details (open ticket).
- Multi-blank cards: region index vs micro-card; interim is per-blank judgment.
- Attachment storage and display: "where the bytes live, how they are referenced, and what the pack supplies versus what the learner supplies is undecided."
- Retention and forgetting: when a card leaves the deck.
- Error and absence: full handling beyond the plain-words rules already locked.
- Categories may still be identifying; generalisation, coarsening, or bounding undecided.
- Onboarding polish, notifications, streaks: "named but not designed" — and streaks on this app's rules can only ever be *named*, not shown.
- The judge seam: many valid answers (equivalence) — undecided; free recall costs by default.
