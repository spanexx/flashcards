GRILL: Learner delight
Status: Locked
Date: 2026-10-25
Pack: learner-delight
Authority: the learner's dictation this session, quoted verbatim below.

Source question, quoted from the learner:
"There's just something missing with the flashcards. It's not like—it's not
catching my attention, it's not animated, it's not colorful, it's not
addictive. You know, users need to love seeing it. And also, I think we don't
have a light mode; we only have that darkmode? there are no optional sound
efects"

Q1. What is actually missing today?
A1. Both palettes already exist, but the app has no in-app theme control: it
    follows the operating system, so a dark desktop can never show the light
    palette. There is no sound code at all. Only one 180ms reveal animation
    exists. The card is readable, but it sits still.

Q2. What does "addictive" mean here, when the app bans score, streak, timer,
rank and comparison?
A2. Delight, not pressure. Motion, colour and sound make the card feel alive,
    but nothing adds a score, streak, timer, rank, notification or guilt.
    "Stopping is not an ending." stays true.

Q3. How far does the look go?
A3. Warm and lively, the learner's chosen option: keep the paper-and-ink
    identity and the one calm column. Add outcome colour washes, a soft accent
    aura, lively pips, a card entrance and a small finish celebration. No
    gradient card face, no confetti storm, no deck textures, no new art assets.

Q4. Which themes exist, and where is the choice?
A4. Light, Dark and System. System is the default. A three-way control sits in
    Settings > Profile under "Appearance", and a one-click sun/moon button sits
    in the sidebar. The choice is device-local (localStorage), because it
    describes this screen, not the learner's data; it is never in a backup.

Q5. When does sound play?
A5. Off by default, the learner's chosen option. One toggle in Settings >
    Profile. Short synthesized cues (Web Audio, no audio files) for a correct
    answer, a nearly answer, a miss, and a completed session. No music, no
    voice, and nothing plays on load.

Q6. What must not move?
A6. The one calm column; no score, streak, timer, rank or comparison; the
    teaching layer's level rules, cold repeat and one-return rule; the judge,
    outcomes, evidence rows, queue, hints, offer algorithm and session ending;
    offline use; and reduced-motion users get no animation.

Q7. What about quiet spaces and motion sensitivity?
A7. Sound stays off until the learner turns it on, and the cues are short and
    quiet. Every new animation is removed under prefers-reduced-motion, and the
    theme control never changes layout.

Q8. Does the theme follow the system when System is chosen?
A8. Yes. The boot script resolves System to the OS preference before first
    paint, and a live listener re-resolves when the OS changes. Choosing Light
    or Dark pins that palette until the learner changes it.
