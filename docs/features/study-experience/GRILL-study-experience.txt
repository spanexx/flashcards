Q1. What exactly leaks the answer before the learner answers?
A1. The `card.summary` chip at the top of every card is often the answer itself
    (for example "Dzień dobry" on a card asking for that phrase). It is removed
    before judging. A neutral mode label replaces it. The summary appears only
    after the answer is revealed.

Q2. What should a listening card show before the answer?
A2. Only a play control and one instruction: "Listen, then type what you hear."
    No summary, no full line, no translation. The duplicate "Play it again" and
    "Hear it" controls become one clear play button.

Q3. What should the tutor actually do when it is on?
A3. The plan says the tutor will offer a hint after a missed answer. When the
    learner turns it on, the first wrong answer on a card automatically asks for
    one tutor hint. The learner can still ask for hints manually. Every hint is
    labelled "Tutor hint" or "Pack hint" so the source is visible. If the tutor
    cannot answer, the pack hint is used and the reason is shown. Automatic help
    is once per card per session, not a stream.

Q4. How does pack choice work?
A4. The plan screen gets a "Study from" selector: All cards (default) or one
    installed pack. Choosing a pack rebuilds the plan from that pack. If the pack
    has decks, a deck selector narrows it further. An empty scope says so and
    offers a way back to All cards. The offer count still works inside the scope.

Q5. What changes about the card's look?
A5. The study card becomes the visual centre: a progress bar, a clear mode label,
    a larger prompt, a distinct answer block, translations on their own line, and
    a calm outcome badge. The four judgement-correction buttons move behind a
    small "Was this fair?" disclosure so the next action is obvious. The layout
    stays comfortable on a phone.
    [Superseded 2026-10-25 by docs/features/study-tab-redesign/: position pips
    replace the progress bar, and the mode label is quiet uppercase text rather
    than a chip.]

Q6. What does not change?
A6. The four outcome words, the judge, the one-return rule, the session offer and
    the no-score ending are untouched. No new dependency. No change to the card
    schema, backup schema, pack schema or provider prompt contract.

Q7. What proves it?
A7. Router tests for pack/deck plan filtering; a study simulation through the
    real router and built output; an external Edge lane that chooses a pack,
    checks no answer text is visible before submitting, checks a listening card
    prints no answer, asks for a hint, sees an automatic tutor hint after a miss,
    and checks the reveal and phone width. Full typecheck, lint, build and test
    suite stay green.
