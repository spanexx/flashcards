No answer on top

The summary chip is hidden until the answer is revealed.

What changed

The old screen printed “Piję kawę” in a chip above the question. That is the answer. The mode label tells the learner what kind of card it is without telling them the words.

The answer appears only after the learner submits.

3

A listening card only asks you to listen

One play button. No summary, no full line, no translation.

Listen and typeCard 4 of 5 · tutor on

Listen, then type what you hear.

What changed

The old screen showed “Gdzie jest dworzec?” above the play buttons. A listening card must not print the line it asks the learner to hear.

After the answer, the line and its translation are revealed.

4

The tutor does something

When it is on, the first miss on a card gets a tutor hint automatically.

NoYou wrote “jade”; expected “jedliśmy”.
My jedliśmy obiad.We were eating dinner.

Tutor hint

The verb is past tense, masculine plural: -liśmy. “Jade” is present tense.

Was this fair?

What changed

Turning the tutor on now has a visible effect: it offers one hint after the first wrong answer on a card. The learner can still ask for more.

Every hint says where it came from: Tutor hint or Pack hint. If the tutor cannot answer, the pack hint is used and the reason is shown.
