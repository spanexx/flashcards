Status: Locked

What this feature is about

- Ship a built-in pack that teaches one Polish word, biuro, as a complete worked example.
- Keep it inside the locked word-lessons model: one word record, five stage cards, Dictionary pages.
- Make the files a pattern another author can copy for a different word.
- Do NOT add new word fields, new answer surfaces, new page levels or game mechanics.
- Do NOT create the pack through the browser UI; it must be authored in v1/packs and seeded into the static export.

4. "Research says a full word lesson covers form, meaning and use: the word's sound and shape, what it means, and how it behaves with other words (Nation, and the word-vs-sentence studies). Our locked word record only has lemma, meaning, forms with a why, and matching pairs. Should this exemplar pack stay inside that locked shape, or add new fields?"
Answer:
- Stay inside the locked shape.
- Put all the richness into existing fields: the form labels and `why` text, the `use` sentences, the pairs, the card prompts and the Dictionary pages.

Why:
- Extending the schema would change the study screen, importer and Dictionary renderer, which is a different, larger pack.
- The existing five stages already give recognition, production, form recall and matching.
- Source: docs/features/word-lessons/PRD-TRD-word-lessons.md sections 2-5.
